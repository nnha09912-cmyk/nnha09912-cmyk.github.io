Books Photo

Fast, compact photo culling for macOS.

Books Photo is a macOS desktop app for photographers. It helps you cull, rate, color-label, sort, rename and sync large RAW + JPG shoots quickly.

Google Drive

Books Photo can connect to your Google Drive so you can browse your folders, preview photos, and upload or download photos you choose. It only acts on your Google Drive when you ask it to.
