Privacy Policy

Books Photo · Last updated: October 20, 2026

Books Photo ("the app") is a desktop application for macOS, made by Tran Nhat Duy. This policy explains what happens to your data, including data from your Google account.

What the app accesses

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app asks for permission to view, create, edit and delete files in your Google Drive (the Google Drive scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive), and to read your email address and basic profile (to show which account is connected).

The app uses this access only to do what you ask: browse your folders, preview photos, upload photos, download photos, rename, move, or move files to the Drive trash. It does nothing in the background that you did not start.

Where your data goes

  • The app runs entirely on your computer. We do not operate a server that receives your Google data, and your files and Drive contents are never sent to us.
  • Sign-in tokens are stored in the macOS Keychain on your own Mac. The app talks directly from your computer to Google's servers.
  • Thumbnails and previews of your Drive photos may be cached on your own computer so they load faster. You can delete this cache at any time.
  • We do not sell, rent, share, or transfer your Google user data to anyone, and we do not use it for advertising.
  • No person at Books Photo reads your Google Drive data.

Google API Services User Data Policy

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Your choices

  • Disconnect an account at any time from the app (Google Drive → account menu → Disconnect). This revokes access and deletes its tokens from your Keychain.
  • You can also remove the app's access in your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Photos on your computer

Ratings, color labels and other culling information are stored in a local database on your Mac. They are not uploaded anywhere unless you copy or upload files yourself.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
